# Daily journal question bank

Choose exactly one question for the day's `Reflection` section. Use the wording below verbatim. Before choosing, inspect the five most recent applicable journal entries and exclude their reflection questions.

## Direction

- What felt worth my time today?
- What do I want more of in my life?
- What am I currently moving toward?
- Did today look like the kind of life I want?
- What would I like to be better at a year from now?

## Habits

- What good habit was easiest today?
- What bad habit happened almost automatically?
- What triggered me to waste time today?
- What could I make easier for myself tomorrow?
- What should I make harder to access?

## Learning

- What did I learn today?
- What made me curious today?
- What do I want to understand better?
- What did I consume that was actually useful?
- What should I learn next instead of endlessly researching?

## Creating and work

- What did I make progress on?
- What did I create instead of consume?
- What interesting thing could I build?
- What opportunity did I notice?
- What am I avoiding because it feels difficult?
- What would make me noticeably better at what I do?

## Attention

- Where did my attention go today?
- What distracted me most?
- When was I most focused?
- What did I spend too much time on?
- What could I have simply ignored?
- What would I rather have done instead of scrolling?

## Identity

- What kind of person was I today?
- What action today made me respect myself more?
- What action was inconsistent with who I want to become?
- What would the version of me I admire have done differently?
- What is one small vote I can cast for that identity tomorrow?
